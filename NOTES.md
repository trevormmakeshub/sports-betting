This repo predicts soccer home, draw, and away results and over/under goal totals.

Completed matches were pulled from football-data.co.uk on 2026-11-07. data/soccer/modelling has 176 league files and 55569 finals from 2019-07-26 through 2026-11-04. The current season is stored as year 2027, the season's ending year. fixtures.csv has 140 matches from 2026-11-07 through 2026-11-09. Team form is the mean of earlier matches in that league and season. Adjusted goals add the published cards, shots on target, and corners when that file includes them. The deleted GitHub data branch is not used. No sportsbook page was scraped. This does not price a live bet.
